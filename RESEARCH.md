## What this means for the design
- **Forgetting is the main failure**, so Saathi gives a single reminder with a single button, not a complex app.
- **Many medicines at once**, so tablets are drawn the way they look in the strip, and doses are grouped by time of day (`1–0–1`).
- **Families live apart**, so the caregiver's view is reassurance first, with an alert only when a dose is actually late.
- **Health data is sensitive**, so the elder gives consent and the encryption is end-to-end.
